01. About Me

Hi, I'm MJ Atkinson, a developer based in Windhoek, Namibia. I currently work in networking and security while studying towards a BSc in Computer Science with Software Engineering at the Namibia University of Science and Technology (NUST).

Growing up playing video games sparked a curiosity for how technology works — and that curiosity never left. Today I channel it into building websites and mobile apps that are practical, clean, and built around real people's needs. My goal is simple: to use technology to make everyday life a little easier for the people around me.
