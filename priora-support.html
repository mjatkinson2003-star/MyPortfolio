Getting started

How do I install Priora?

Search for "Priora" in the Google Workspace Marketplace and click Install. Once installed, open Gmail and click the Priora icon in the right sidebar to get started.

Why does Priora need access to my Gmail and Calendar?

Priora reads your inbox and calendar to build your weekly brief. It only reads email subject lines and sender names — not the full content of every email. Calendar access is needed to show your week and book events when you tap a booking button.

How do I set up the automatic weekly trigger?

Open Priora in Gmail, tap "Settings — schedule my brief" on the homepage, choose your preferred day and time, then tap "Save and activate". Priora will email you your brief automatically every week at that time.

How do I book a calendar event through Priora?

Tap "Analyse my week" to load your weekly brief. Under "Quick actions" you'll see booking buttons for a focus block, deep work block, and out-of-office. Tap any button to instantly add the event to your Google Calendar.

The AI summary isn't loading — what do I do?

Try tapping "Refresh" at the bottom of the brief. If it still doesn't load, close and reopen the Priora sidebar. If the issue persists, contact us at mjatkinsondigital@gmail.com.

How do I remove Priora from my Google account?

Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, find Priora in the list, and click "Remove access". You can also uninstall it from the Google Workspace Marketplace.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through the Google Workspace Marketplace. No cancellation fees apply.
