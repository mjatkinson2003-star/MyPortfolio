Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Effective date: May 2026

Priora ("we", "our", or "us") is a Gmail add-on developed by Atkinson Digital. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Priora.

1. Information we access

Priora requests access to the following Google data to provide its service:

  • Gmail: We read your unread email threads to identify meeting requests, urgent items, and action items. We do not read the full body of every email — only subject lines and sender information unless needed for AI analysis.
  • Google Calendar: We read your calendar events for the current week to provide scheduling context. We create calendar events only when you explicitly tap a booking button.

We do not access, store, or transmit any other Google data beyond what is listed above.

2. How we use your data

Your data is used solely to provide the Priora service. Specifically:

  • Email subject lines and sender names are sent to the Google Gemini API to generate your AI weekly brief.
  • Calendar event titles and times are used to build your weekly summary.
  • No data is stored on our servers. All processing happens in real time and is not retained after your session.

3. Third-party services

Priora uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Gemini API: Used to generate AI-written summaries from your inbox and calendar data. Data sent to Gemini is subject to Google's API Terms of Service.

4. Data storage and retention

Priora does not store your emails, calendar events, or any personal data on external servers. Your trigger preferences (day and time settings) are stored locally within your Google account's Apps Script user properties and are not accessible to us.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties except as described in Section 3 above (Google Gemini API for AI processing).

6. Your rights

You may revoke Priora's access to your Google account at any time by visiting myaccount.google.com/permissions and removing Priora from the list of connected apps.

7. Children's privacy

Priora is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
